## Context

By 2025 I was a Senior Analyst at Capgemini, two and a half years in, running 50
or more incidents a day against SLA and administering Active Directory and
endpoint compliance across enterprise environments. The role was stable and I was
good at it.

Two things had changed underneath that. I had been studying through the IIIT
Bangalore Data Science programme alongside the job, which finished with the NLP
specialisation in October 2025, so the interest was two years old rather than
sudden. And I had built the access-request tool described in
[001](001-human-approval-before-directory-writes.md). That tool was the first
time the work felt like engineering a system rather than operating one, and I
wanted considerably more of that than the role contained.

## Decision

Resign in December 2025 and move to London for an MSc in Artificial Intelligence
and Computer Science at Northeastern University London.

## Alternatives considered

**Stay and move internally.** Capgemini is large and has ML work in it. The
honest reason I did not pursue this: internal moves from operations into
engineering are slow and usually need the credential I did not have. I would have
spent two years waiting for permission.

**Keep studying part time and stay employed.** Cheaper and lower risk, and it is
what I had already been doing for two years. It had produced certificates rather
than shipped systems, which told me the constraint was time and not motivation.

**Self-study and apply directly.** No tuition, no visa cost, no relocation. I
rejected it because I had no production ML to point at, and without either a
credential or a portfolio the applications go nowhere. The MSc buys the
credential; this assistant is meant to be the portfolio half.

## Consequences

The cost is real: a salary given up, tuition, and a student visa that permits
20 hours a week in term time rather than full-time work until the course
completes in January 2027.

What it buys is time to build things that can be measured, and a dissertation in
agentic AI and LLMs that sits on the same ground as the work I want to be hired
for. The thing I am still exposed to is the obvious one: three years of
production operations is not three years of production ML, and I say so rather
than blur it.
